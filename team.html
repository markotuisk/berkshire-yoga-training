The people behind Thames Wellness Academy

Experienced educators, studio leaders and clinical specialists committed to credible, teacher-led wellness training across Berkshire.

Co-founders

Building a leading local wellness training centre

&
Women-owned

Both Raili Maripuu and Katia Major are entrepreneurs, owners of multiple successful businesses, directors and founders.

Besides being women in business, they are uniquely skilled individuals who now wish to share what they have been learning over decades of wisdom and CPD.

Co-founder · Senior Ashtanga teacher · Goyoga Tallinn

Raili Maripuu

Studio founder, Yoga Pros Senior Yoga Teacher and Lino Miele-recognised Ashtanga educator. Learn more at railimaripuu.com.
