How to become a yoga teacher

To become a yoga teacher in the UK, build a consistent personal practice, complete an accredited teacher-training qualification, then obtain insurance, first-aid certification, and practical teaching experience.

Typical UK route

Practise regularly. Most training providers expect a sustained personal practice before enrolment. Be familiar with common classes, breath work, and safe movement.

Choose an accredited course. Common routes include a 200-hour Yoga Teacher Training, a British Wheel of Yoga diploma, or a regulated Level 3/4 Diploma in Teaching Yoga.

View the 200-hour course

Complete training and assessments. Courses typically cover asana, pranayama, anatomy and physiology, yoga philosophy, sequencing, class planning, safety, and supervised teaching practice.

Meet professional requirements. Secure public-liability and professional-indemnity insurance plus a recognised first-aid and CPR certificate. Many studios and gyms expect these before you can teach.

Before you teach

Insurance and first aid

Public-liability and professional-indemnity insurance, plus a recognised first-aid and CPR certificate.

Build teaching experience. Start with community, online, cover, or studio classes, and consider registering with a professional body such as the Register of Exercise Professionals to demonstrate credibility to employers and clients.

Before enrolling

Ask Thames Wellness Academy for:

  • The exact qualification awarded, total contact hours, and whether it is equivalent to or includes a 200-hour teacher-training standard.
  • Course dates, format, venue, cost, payment plans, and cancellation terms.
  • Assessment requirements, including observed teaching practice and written or practical exams.
  • Whether graduates receive support with insurance, first aid, mentoring, or finding teaching work.
  • Confirmation of current accreditation and how it compares with Yoga Alliance Professionals, British Wheel of Yoga, or a regulated Level 3/4 qualification.

There is no single statutory licence for teaching yoga in the UK. A credible accredited qualification, insurance, first-aid training, and supervised teaching experience are the practical foundations for working professionally.
