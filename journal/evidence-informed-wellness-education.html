Evidence-informed wellness education

Why research collaboration matters for credible teacher training and CPD programmes at Thames Wellness Academy.

Credible wellness education balances lived teaching experience with evidence from anatomy, physiology and applied research. That balance protects students and strengthens professional trust.

What evidence-informed means here

It means curriculum that is updated with current understanding, faculty who can explain why a method is used, and assessment that values safe practice over trends.

Where this shows up in courses

Foundation Training and CPD modules include anatomy depth and supervised teaching practice so graduates can apply learning with real people. Explore courses or read about our studio partners.
