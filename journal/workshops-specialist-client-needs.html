Workshop highlights and practitioner tips

Insights from specialist workshops on sleep health, menopause support and trauma-informed teaching.

Specialist workshops help teachers adapt for diverse client needs without abandoning foundations. Themes such as sleep health, menopause support and trauma-informed teaching require care, clarity and professional boundaries.

Takeaways for class craft

  • Offer choice and pacing options
  • Use plain language for safety cues
  • Know when to refer on rather than improvise
