Overview

CPD for wellness professionals

Our CPD programmes support teachers, therapists and corporate wellbeing practitioners in deepening their expertise and staying current with movement science, therapeutic practice and holistic wellbeing.

Each course is delivered on site by senior educators and subject matter specialists across Berkshire and Buckinghamshire, ensuring high-quality training that enhances both professional competence and client outcomes.

Enquire about CPD
How long are CPD courses?

CPD courses vary from single-day intensives to modular short programmes. We will confirm duration and dates when you enquire.

Do I need to be a qualified teacher?

Most CPD courses are designed for qualified teachers or experienced wellbeing practitioners. Entry requirements are stated per course.

Where is CPD delivered?

Courses are delivered on site across Berkshire and Buckinghamshire. Venues are confirmed per course schedule.

Is CPD certified?

CPD aligns with Yoga Alliance Professionals continuing education standards where applicable. Certification details are provided per course.
