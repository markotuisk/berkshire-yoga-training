Foundation Training

Internationally recognised teacher training from 200-hour Foundation to 500-hour Advanced level. Delivered on site across Berkshire and Buckinghamshire.

Overview

Train to teach with confidence

Our Teacher Training programmes offer Yoga Alliance Professionals certification for aspiring and experienced teachers. Courses blend anatomy, physiology and biomechanics with mindfulness, breath work and holistic wellbeing principles.

Trainees gain a solid foundation in safe, effective teaching, together with the tools to support diverse student needs across corporate, studio and therapeutic settings.

Led by senior educators

Supported by clinical specialists, our training combines academic rigour with personal growth, preparing graduates to deliver transformative, evidence-informed wellness experiences in person.

Curriculum

What you will learn

Foundation Training questions

Common questions about certification, delivery and enrolment. Contact us for programme-specific advice.

Ask a question
Is the training certified by Yoga Alliance Professionals?

Yes. Our Foundation Training pathways align with Yoga Alliance Professionals standards for internationally recognised certification.

Do I need prior teaching experience?

The 200-hour pathway is designed for aspiring teachers. The 300-hour and 500-hour pathways suit teachers building on existing certification and experience.

Is training delivered online or in person?

Our programmes are teacher-led and delivered on site across Berkshire and Buckinghamshire. This is not a pre-recorded online-only course.

How long does each pathway take?

Duration varies by pathway and study format. We will outline timelines during your enquiry based on your chosen level and availability.

Can I work while I train?

Many trainees combine study with professional commitments. Modular and weekend formats are designed to support working practitioners.
