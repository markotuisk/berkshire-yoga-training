About

Raili Maripuu is co-founder of Thames Wellness Academy and brings studio leadership from Goyoga Tallinn into the academy's teacher education work. Her practice spans Tallinn and wider European teaching contexts, with a clear interest in long-form training rather than one-off workshops alone.

That studio leadership informs how the Academy designs cohorts: sustained contact with faculty, structured progression, and attention to how teachers grow over time. Raili's European perspective sits alongside Berkshire delivery, connecting Thames Wellness Academy to a broader professional community.

Together with Katia Major, she co-founded the Academy to raise standards of further education for the wellness sector. The shared brief is straightforward: teacher-led programmes, professional credibility, and learning that holds up in real studios and workplaces.

Enquirers can follow her wider work through Goyoga Tallinn and her professional site, while academy programmes remain the primary route into Thames Wellness Academy training in Berkshire and Reading.

Qualifications and affiliations

  • Co-founder, Thames Wellness Academy
  • Studio leadership through Goyoga Tallinn
  • Long-form teacher education across Europe
