Katia Major

About

After more than 14 years in corporate education, managing large contracts and delivering programmes in the UK and abroad, Katia made a deliberate move into health and wellbeing. She had long believed that movement is one of the most powerful ways to improve quality of life, and had felt its effects first-hand through running, strength training and yoga.

She qualified as a yoga teacher in 2014 and played a key part in growing Yoga Reading into a well-established community studio. It now offers classes, workshops, retreats, and wellbeing programmes for workplaces and schools across Greater Reading. In 2018 she added a Level 5 Soft Tissue Therapy qualification and a Level 3 Personal Training Diploma, building her skills in rehabilitation, injury prevention and exercise prescription.

Her approach rests on movement optimism: the belief that getting people moving, and enjoying it, brings lasting benefits to body and mind.

Katia loves working with like-minded businesses and entrepreneurs who want wellbeing to be genuinely accessible. Through Thames Health and Wellness, she brings clinical know-how, professional credibility and plenty of enthusiasm to help individuals and organisations build healthier, more energised lives.
