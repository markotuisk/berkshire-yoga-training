About

Lorena Rodrigo is a yoga teacher, sound meditation practitioner and Reiki Master based in Reading, Berkshire, and an academy teacher at Thames Wellness Academy. Originally from Spain, she has lived in the UK since 2000. Her work brings together yoga, sound, breathwork and energy practices, creating thoughtful spaces for people to reconnect with themselves through movement, stillness and sound.

Yoga has been a constant thread throughout Lorena's life, eventually inspiring her to pursue teacher training in 2015. She completed her first training in Ashtanga Vinyasa Yoga before deepening her studies in 2017 with Anusara Elements Yoga. Her continuing education spans Yin, Pregnancy Yoga, Restorative practices and Yoga for Cancer Survivors, alongside studies in anatomy, yoga therapy and breathwork.

Alongside yoga, Lorena has developed a dedicated sound meditation practice, working with gongs, singing bowls, drums, chimes and other resonant instruments. Her sessions combine carefully curated soundscapes with guided relaxation and breath awareness, inviting participants into a space of deep rest, sensory exploration and connection. Her approach is intuitive and heart-led, with each sound journey shaped around the people, setting and intention of the session.

Lorena is the founder of SOMA (Sound, Oneness, Movement and Well-being Arts), through which she offers yoga classes, sound meditations, Reiki sessions, workshops and retreats. She works with individuals, groups and organisations, collaborating with studios, retreat venues and wellness events across Berkshire, Oxfordshire and London.

Her own life experiences have profoundly influenced her teaching, strengthening her appreciation for the role that movement, rest and self-care can play in navigating life's challenges. Today, she is passionate about sharing accessible, nourishing practices that support nervous system regulation, embodied awareness and a deeper relationship with oneself.

Teaching and specialties

Lorena's teaching combines traditional yoga foundations with an exploratory, embodied approach to movement and sound. She creates welcoming environments where participants can develop greater body awareness, explore breath and experience the restorative qualities of stillness.

  • Anusara yoga
  • Yin, restorative and pregnancy yoga
  • Yoga for Cancer Survivors
  • Sound meditation, gong baths and immersive sound journeys
  • Singing bowls and sound meditation education
  • Breathwork and relaxation practices
  • Reiki and Seichim
  • Workshops, retreats and bespoke wellbeing experiences
