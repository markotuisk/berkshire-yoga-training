About

Lorena Rodrigo is an academy teacher at Thames Wellness Academy, supporting teacher-led programmes across Berkshire. She works within the academy's faculty model: on-site delivery, clear cohort structure, and attention to how trainees practise in the room.

Her role sits alongside the co-founders and fellow educators who shape Foundation Training, CPD and related pathways. The emphasis is on practical teaching craft, professional conduct and the day-to-day feedback that helps new and developing teachers improve.

Berkshire remains the primary setting for her academy work. Students meet her through scheduled programme sessions rather than remote-only modules, in line with the Academy's preference for people over platforms.

A fuller biography, photograph refresh and verified qualification list will be expanded on this page as additional faculty details are published. Until then, this profile records her confirmed role and location within Thames Wellness Academy.
