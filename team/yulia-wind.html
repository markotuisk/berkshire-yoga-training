About

Yulia Wind is an academy teacher at Thames Wellness Academy, contributing to teacher-led programmes across Berkshire. She works as part of the faculty team that delivers training in person, with the Academy's standards for professional wellness education in view.

Her teaching supports trainees as they move through academy pathways: listening in class, practising safely, and building the habits expected of credible educators. The work is grounded in Berkshire delivery and the academy's preference for small cohorts and faculty attention.

Yulia's profile sits with fellow academy teachers and co-founders who share responsibility for programme quality. Enquirers looking for course detail should follow Foundation Training, CPD and workshop pages, then apply or contact the Academy directly.

Further biographical detail and verified qualifications will be published here as they are confirmed. This page currently records her established role as an academy teacher based in Berkshire.
