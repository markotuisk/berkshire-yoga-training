In development

Retreat programmes launching soon

Our retreat programmes are in development. We are designing immersive experiences that combine the academic rigour of Thames Wellness Academy with the restorative benefits of focused time away from daily routines.

Retreats will be teacher-led, grounded in evidence-informed practice, and held in settings across Berkshire and Buckinghamshire that support reflection, practicum and community learning.

When will retreats be available?

Retreat programmes are in development. Register your interest and we will notify you when dates and venues are confirmed.

Will retreats be open to the public?

Some retreats will be open to practitioners and advanced students. Others may be designed for specific cohorts within our training programmes.

Where will retreats be held?

We plan to host retreats in Berkshire and Buckinghamshire, in venues suited to restorative learning and on-site practice.

How do I register interest?

Contact us via the form on our contact page or email info@thameswellnessacademy.co.uk with your name and area of interest.
