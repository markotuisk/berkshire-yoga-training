Overview

Focused professional development

Our specialist workshops offer immersive training in focused areas of wellness, ideal for practitioners seeking to broaden their skill set or add niche services to their offerings.

Practical, hands-on, and rooted in both modern research and traditional wisdom, these workshops equip professionals with tools to support clients at a deeper level and deliver more targeted, personalised wellbeing experiences.

  • Sleep health
  • Hormone balance
  • Menopause support
  • Breath work
  • Trauma-informed teaching
  • First Aid

Enquire about workshops
Who can attend workshops?

Workshops are designed for qualified teachers, therapists and wellbeing practitioners. Some workshops may have specific prerequisites.

How do workshops differ from CPD?

Workshops focus on a single specialist theme in depth. CPD courses often cover broader professional development topics over a slightly longer format.

Where are workshops held?

We deliver on site across Berkshire and Buckinghamshire. Venue and dates are confirmed per workshop.

Can I book for my team?

Yes. Contact us to discuss corporate or group bookings for workplace wellbeing teams.
