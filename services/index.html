Services at TWA

Wellness education, delivered on site

Thames Wellness Academy offers teacher-led programmes for aspiring yoga teachers, qualified practitioners and organisations across Berkshire and Buckinghamshire. Every service is Yoga Alliance Professionals certified or aligned, and delivered in person across studio, corporate and therapeutic settings.

Your learning journey

How our services connect

Our four offerings form a clear progression. Begin with accredited teacher training, deepen your expertise through CPD, explore specialist workshops, and gather for immersive retreats. Each programme stands alone, yet they are designed to complement one another as your practice evolves.

1. Foundation Training

Build your teaching credentials from 200-hour Foundation through to 500-hour Advanced level with Yoga Alliance Professionals certification.

2. CPD

Stay current with short, on-site courses in anatomy, mobility, pain-aware teaching and professional wellbeing for qualified practitioners.

3. Workshops

Go deeper in focused areas such as breath work, sleep health, menopause support and trauma-informed teaching through hands-on specialist sessions.

4. Retreats

Step away for restorative, teacher-led experiences that combine in-depth learning with space to reflect and reconnect. Programmes in development.
