About this course

The Academy lists Yoga Nidra Training as a 30-hour CPD pathway. The April 2027 cohort is delivered across seven modules on five session days: four studio days and one self-study block. Studio teaching takes place in Berkshire and Reading; the exact venue is confirmed on enrolment.

Who it is for

Qualified yoga and wellness teachers who want to teach Yoga Nidra and restorative rest with a professional structure.
