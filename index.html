FAQ

Before you apply

Clear answers reduce friction. Full FAQ page includes schema for rich results where eligible.

Is Thames Wellness Academy the same as Berkshire Yoga Training?

Yes. Thames Wellness Academy is the Academy brand. Berkshire Yoga Training remains the public domain and trading name on the live site while domains are aligned.

Are programmes delivered online or on site?

Core programmes are teacher-led and delivered on site across Berkshire and the wider Thames Valley. They are not app-based or fully automated.

What certification do graduates receive?

Foundation Training pathways are designed for Yoga Alliance Professionals recognition, subject to programme completion requirements.

How do I apply or enquire?

Use the Apply page to tell us your goals. We will help you choose Foundation Training, CPD or workshops.
