About us

Credible wellness education, rooted in the Thames Valley

Thames Wellness Academy is a women-owned training organisation delivering Yoga Alliance Professionals-certified programmes across Berkshire.

Academy training environment
Our story

Born from friendship, built on experience

Raili Maripuu and Katia Major founded Thames Wellness Academy through a shared commitment to credible, teacher-led wellness education. Both had spent years building respected studio communities: Raili through Goyoga Tallinn and senior Ashtanga teaching, Katia through Yoga Reading, clinical soft tissue practice and anatomy-informed teaching in Berkshire.

They saw a gap in the market for training that combined academic rigour with practical, on-site teaching skills. Not an app, not pre-recorded modules, but structured further education led by experienced educators who understand what it takes to teach safely across studio, corporate and therapeutic settings.

Together, they set out to create a leading local wellness training centre: one that raises standards, supports practitioners at every career stage, and serves the Thames Valley community with integrity.

Our mission

Raising the standard of wellness education

We deliver high-quality further education and certified professional development for the wellness sector, with teacher-led, on-site programmes that prepare graduates for real-world teaching across diverse settings.

Certified by

Our mission is to blend anatomy, physiology and biomechanics with mindfulness, breath work and holistic wellbeing principles, so every graduate leaves with the knowledge, confidence and supervised practicum experience to teach safely and effectively.

We believe wellness education should be evidence-informed, inclusive and delivered by people who have spent years in practice, not just in theory.

Core values

What guides everything we do

Credibility

Yoga Alliance Professionals certification, clinical specialists on faculty, and curricula grounded in evidence-informed movement science.

Teacher-led excellence

Structured, in-person sessions with senior educators. Supervised practicum, not passive online modules.

Inclusivity

Training designed for diverse populations and settings: studio, corporate, therapeutic and specialist client needs.

Community

Small cohorts, collaborative learning and ongoing support from enquiry through to graduation and beyond.

Integrity

Transparent standards, ethical teaching practice and honest communication about what our programmes deliver.

Growth

Personal and professional development woven into every pathway, from Foundation Training through to advanced CPD.

Milestones

Our journey so far

From established studio practice to a dedicated academy serving the Thames Valley.

  1. 2014

    Foundations in teaching

    Katia Major qualifies as a yoga teacher and begins building Yoga Reading, a studio community serving Caversham and Berkshire.

  2. 2020

    Studio leadership

    Katia becomes sole director of Yoga Reading, expanding clinical support and anatomy-informed teaching. Raili continues senior Ashtanga practice and studio leadership in Tallinn and Berkshire through Goyoga Tallinn.

  3. 2024

    The academy concept

    Raili and Katia formalise their shared vision: a women-owned academy delivering credible, teacher-led wellness education across the Thames Valley.

  4. 2025

    Yoga Alliance Professionals pathway

    Thames Wellness Academy establishes certified Foundation Training, CPD and specialist workshop programmes with supervised practicum and clinical faculty input.

  5. 2026

    Academy launch

    Full programme portfolio goes live across Berkshire and Buckinghamshire, with research collaboration, journal content and retreat programmes in development.

Our identity

The name and colours behind TWA

Why Thames Wellness Academy?

Thames honours the River Thames and the Thames Valley region where we train practitioners across Berkshire, Buckinghamshire and surrounding areas. Our roots are local, our standards are international.

Wellness reflects our holistic scope: anatomy, physiology, breath work, mindfulness and therapeutic practice, not yoga alone.

Academy signals structured further education and professional development, not casual drop-in classes. We are building a centre of excellence for teacher-led training.

Our colour palette

Every colour choice communicates who we are: professional, warm and focused on human connection.

  • Golden accent Warmth, vitality and the transformative energy of mindful movement
  • Charcoal Authority, clarity and professional credibility
  • Clean neutrals Calm, focused learning environments that put people first
Future training centre vision
The future

What our founders are building

Raili and Katia have seeded a long-term vision into Thames Wellness Academy: a dedicated wellness training centre in Berkshire that serves as a hub for credible education, research collaboration and community.

  • A permanent academy home with purpose-built training spaces and clinical teaching facilities
  • Expanded faculty of senior educators, clinical specialists and therapeutic practitioners
  • Research partnerships with universities and colleges across the Thames Valley
  • Immersive retreat programmes combining academic rigour with restorative practice
  • Corporate and therapeutic training networks connecting Berkshire studios with international communities

This is not a side project. It is the next chapter of two careers spent building trust, teaching with integrity and raising standards in wellness education.
