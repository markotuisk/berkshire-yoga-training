Our story

Credible wellness education, rooted in the Thames Valley

Thames Wellness Academy is a women-owned training organisation delivering Yoga Alliance Professionals-certified programmes across Berkshire and Reading.

Who we are

We exist to raise the standard of further education for the wellness sector. Our programmes combine academic rigour with personal growth, preparing graduates for safe, effective teaching across corporate, studio and therapeutic settings.

Co-founded by Raili Maripuu of Goyoga Tallinn and Katia Major of Yoga Reading, the Academy draws on decades of studio leadership and clinical practice across Berkshire, Tallinn and beyond.

Trading publicly as Berkshire Yoga Training, we keep a clear brand identity: Thames Wellness Academy for the institution, Berkshire Yoga Training for the live domain while DNS and brand cutover continue.
