Eligibility

Who is it for

Quality training with a robust deep dive into Yoga and sister sciences. Opportunity to develop a new career path.

  • Practitioners ready to deepen technique, anatomy and teaching craft
  • People preparing to teach in studio, corporate or community settings
  • Career changers who want a structured, teacher-led certification route
  • Dedicated students interested in in-depth study and practice, even if teaching is a later goal

What is included

  • 200 hours accreditation: 180 contact hours and 20 self-study hours across techniques, teaching methodology, anatomy, philosophy and practicum
  • Course manual and reading guidance (details confirmed at enrolment)
  • Mentoring and faculty feedback on practice teaching
  • Assessment toward a Yoga Pros foundation pathway credential
  • Clear attendance and homework expectations so you know the workload
  • Accommodation available for out of County/Country students who want to study with us at affordable prices (limited spaces available). See Accommodation.

Outcomes

Graduates leave able to:

  • Plan and teach a coherent mixed-level class with safe progressions
  • Apply foundational anatomy and biomechanics language in cueing and assists
  • Hold basic philosophy and ethics conversations relevant to professional teaching
  • Complete supervised practicum with documented faculty feedback
  • Continue into advanced Foundation hours and Academy CPD with confidence
  • Support into paid teaching roles at local studios across Berkshire. References for out of County students and a road map for how to successfully build a portfolio of work

Questions

FAQ for the 200-hour

On-page answers with FAQ schema. Sitewide FAQ remains available for Academy-wide topics.

Do I need to want to teach, or can I join for personal practice?

Most trainees join to teach. A strong personal-practice motive is welcome if you accept assessment and supervised teaching hours. Tell us your aim when you apply.

What practice experience do you expect?

At least two years of regular yoga practice or consistent class attendance with a recognised teacher, plus readiness for weekend intensives. You do not have to be an advanced practitioner.

Is the course Yoga Pros accredited?

Yes, Foundation Training is designed as a Yoga Professionals Organisation Limited pathway. Credential wording matches the registration status for your cohort.

How many hours are in person versus self-study?

180 contact hours across nine in-studio weekends (09:30-17:00 each day), plus 20 self-study hours in three separate modules. See the schedule and self-study sections.

Can I work full time alongside the course?

Many trainees do. The course runs as nine weekend intensives from February to November 2027, with homework between sessions. Plan recovery time after long modules.

What is the payment plan and is the deposit refundable?

Fees are Early Bird £2,925 and Standard £3,250. Deposit to secure your place: 30% of the fee (Early Bird £877.50 · Standard £975). Payment in instalments is available. Refund and withdrawal rules are as per the Academy Terms and Conditions. Email contact@thameswellness.com for payment plans.

What if I miss a module?

Attendance rules are set before enrolment. Catch-up or later-cohort options are decided with faculty case by case.

How are we assessed?

Attendance, practicum, written or reflective work, and faculty feedback. Full criteria are shared at enrolment.

Where is it held and is parking or public transport easy?

On-site venues across Berkshire and Reading. Parking and rail notes are in your joining pack. See the location page for travel guidance.

How do I apply, and how long until I hear back?

Use Apply or email contact@thameswellness.com. We review enquiries personally and reply with pathway guidance.

Is there a bursary or discount?

Early Bird when available. Ask about partner or alumni considerations. Nothing is discounted until confirmed in writing.

What related training can I take next?

Advanced Foundation hours toward 300 and 500, plus CPD such as Ashtanga, pregnancy, sound, anatomy and Nidra.
