What you will learn

The 200-hour course covers techniques, teaching methodology, anatomy, philosophy and practicum. Exact hour splits are confirmed at enrolment. The dedicated 200-hour page has the full checklist.

  • Asana, sequencing and class craft for mixed-level students
  • Anatomy, physiology and biomechanics for safe teaching
  • Breath work, mindfulness and holistic wellbeing principles
  • Supervised teaching practice with faculty feedback
  • Professional standards aligned to Yoga Alliance Professionals

Who it is for

Aspiring teachers beginning a professional pathway, and practitioners who want a structured, on-site certification route rather than fully automated learning.

Delivery

Sessions are teacher-led and delivered on site across Berkshire. Cohort sizes stay intentional so feedback remains personal.

Fees

200-hour Foundation Teacher Training: Early Bird £2,925 · Standard £3,250. Deposit to secure your place: 30% of the fee (Early Bird £877.50 · Standard £975). Balance due before the course starts; instalment details confirmed in your offer letter. Cohort: 12 February to 14 November 2027.
