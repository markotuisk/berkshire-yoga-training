Eligibility

Who it is for

Invited practitioners with a foundation certificate who want structured Ashtanga assistant development under faculty supervision.

  • Holders of a foundation teacher training certificate
  • People invited by the Academy or studio faculty into this development pathway
  • Assistants building classroom craft in Mysore and led Ashtanga settings
  • Not suitable as an open public CPD booking

There is no public application deadline. Invitation and suitability are confirmed with faculty before a place is offered.

Curriculum

Syllabus

Thirteen modules spanning theory, adjustments, classroom practice, philosophy and assessment. Exact dates for the September 2027 cohort are TBA and will replace the Date TBA column when confirmed.

ModuleFocusHoursDate
1Theoretical · Fundamentals of Ashtanga yoga (Meaning and 8-Limbs, Scientific System, Parampara, Practise Series and Progression, Key Elements)4TBA
2Theoretical and Practical · Breath, Pranayama Techniques, Mantras4TBA
3Theoretical and Adjustments · Primary Series Standing Sequence Vol 1 (Suryanamaskara A to Ardha Baddha Padmottasana)4TBA
4Theoretical and Adjustments · Primary Series Standing and Seated Sequence Vol 1 (Utkatasana to Janusirsasana C)4TBA
5Theoretical and Adjustments · Primary Series Seated Sequence Vol 2 (Marichyasana to Supta Kurmasana)4TBA
6Theoretical and Adjustments · Primary Series Seated Sequence Vol 3 (Garbhapindasana to Setu Bhandasana)4TBA
7Theoretical and Adjustments · Back-bending and Finishing Sequence4TBA
8Theoretical and Practical · Working the Classroom (Mysore and led class; led class up to Navasana with vinyasa count)4TBA
9Theoretical and Philosophy · Introduction to Philosophy via related texts (Bhagavad Gita 2hrs · Yoga Sutras 2hrs)4TBA
10Practical and Adjustments · Run and attend led class up to Navasana (4 × 75 mins) · Attend one full led class with Pranayama (2hrs) · Feedback task for lead teachersSee notesWithin cohort
11Practical and Adjustments · Assist a weekly Mysore class at Yoga Reading (2hrs × 20 weeks)40Within cohort
12Practical · Self-practise at Yoga Reading (20 classes = 33hrs)33Within cohort
13Individual interview (1hr) · Certificate of Completion or Certificate of Attendance1TBA

Modules 3 to 7 include meaning, impact, correct vinyasa, anatomy, modifications, beginners and injuries as published in the course syllabus.

Required bibliography

  • Lino Miele, Ashtanga Yoga (Yoga of Breath), or Petri Räisänen, Ashtanga Yoga: The Primary Series Practise Manual, or another detailed Primary Series manual
  • Gregor Maehle, Ashtanga Yoga: Practise and Philosophy
  • Sri K. Pattabhi Jois, Yoga Mala
  • David Keil, Functional Anatomy of Yoga
  • B.K.S. Iyengar, Light on the Yoga Sutras of Patanjali
  • Eknath Easwaran, The Bhagavad Gita

  • Roopa Pai, The Yoga Sutras for Children
  • Jiddu Krishnamurti, Freedom from the Known

FAQ

Is this a CPD course?

No. It is a Development Course. It sits outside the Academy CPD catalogue.

Can anyone apply online?

A foundation certificate is required. Invitation and suitability are confirmed with faculty before a place is offered.

From September 2027. Exact module dates are TBA and will be shared with invitees and published here when confirmed.

Where is it held?

Is there a Level 2?
