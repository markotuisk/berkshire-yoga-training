Frequently asked questions

Practical answers about courses, certification, delivery and applications at Thames Wellness Academy.

Is Thames Wellness Academy the same as Berkshire Yoga Training?

Yes. Thames Wellness Academy is the Academy brand. Berkshire Yoga Training remains the public domain and trading name on the live site while domains are aligned.

Where are programmes delivered?

Programmes are teacher-led and delivered on site across Berkshire and the wider Thames Valley, including studio, corporate and therapeutic settings.

What is Foundation Training?

Foundation Training is our flagship Yoga Alliance Professionals pathway, spanning 200 to 500-hour routes with supervised teaching practice. See the Foundation Training page.

Do you offer CPD for qualified teachers?

Yes. CPD modules are designed for qualified practitioners who need flexible, evidence-informed professional development.

How do I apply?

Use the Apply page to share your goals and experience. We will help you choose the right course.
