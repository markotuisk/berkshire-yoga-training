Where we train

On-site learning across Berkshire and the Thames Valley

Programmes are teacher-led and delivered on site in studio, corporate and therapeutic settings across Reading, Windsor, Newbury and the wider Thames Valley. Exact venue details are confirmed with each cohort.

How to get here Ask about venues

Training areas we already publish

Thames Wellness Academy (trading as Berkshire Yoga Training) delivers on-site education across Berkshire and Reading. Published delivery areas also include Windsor and Newbury, with teaching heartland in Reading and the surrounding Thames Valley.

We do not publish a single permanent street address on this page yet. Venue addresses, maps and access notes are shared in joining instructions before each programme starts.

  • Reading, Berkshire
  • Windsor, Berkshire
  • Newbury, Berkshire
  • Wider Thames Valley studio, corporate and therapeutic settings

Founding studio partner Yoga Reading supports professional practice in Reading. Contact us if you need access guidance for an upcoming date.

Train

Arriving by train

Reading is our published teaching heartland and a major Thames Valley rail hub, which makes national and regional rail a practical option for many cohorts. Windsor and Newbury venues are also within Berkshire rail catchments when programmes are scheduled there.

[Placeholder] Named stations, walking times from the platform and step-free access notes will be published with each venue. For now, rail arrivals are confirmed in joining instructions for your course dates.
