Professional horizons

Your Opportunities beyond qualification

Training opens doors, but knowing which doors to walk through takes guidance. We connect qualified practitioners and studio partners with teaching placements, corporate programmes and career pathways across the Thames Valley.

Overview

From certification to meaningful work

Many newly qualified teachers struggle to find their first regular classes. Studio owners need credible trainees and staff development. Organisations want wellness programmes delivered by professionals, not generic content providers.

Thames Wellness Academy sits at the intersection of these needs. Through our founding studio partners, faculty network and corporate delivery experience, we help practitioners and organisations create opportunities that are sustainable and professionally sound.

Where our network creates value

Practical pathways for practitioners, studios and organisations seeking credible wellness delivery.

Studio teaching placements

Graduates and advanced trainees can access teaching opportunities through partner studios such as Yoga Reading, with mentor support from senior faculty.

Studio partnership programmes

Studios can host Foundation Training, book team CPD and develop their own teachers through structured programmes delivered on site.

Corporate wellness delivery

Organisations benefit from evidence-informed programmes led by qualified practitioners, covering stress management, mobility and workplace wellbeing.

Specialist workshop facilitation

Practitioners who complete specialist workshops can bring those skills into studio timetables, retreats and one-to-one practice.

International studio connections

FAQs

Opportunities questions

How partnerships and career pathways work in practice.

Start a conversation
Do you guarantee teaching work after graduation?

We do not guarantee placements, but we actively connect graduates with partner studios and corporate opportunities where there is a genuine fit. Many opportunities arise through practicum, CPD and studio relationships built during training.

Can my studio host a teacher training cohort?

Yes. We work with studios across Berkshire and Buckinghamshire to deliver Foundation Training on site. Visit our Partners page or contact us to discuss requirements.

Do you deliver corporate wellness programmes?

We design and deliver on-site corporate wellness sessions led by qualified practitioners. Topics include stress management, mobility and mindful movement for workplace settings.

How do international opportunities work?

Through our founding partnership with Goyoga Tallinn, selected practitioners can access cross-studio learning and professional exchange. Enquire for current opportunities.
