Starting out

Your Path into wellness teaching

A credible route into teaching starts with the right training structure, supervised practice and internationally recognised certification. We help you navigate each step with clarity.

Overview

From first enquiry to qualified teacher

Many people arrive at teacher training from different starting points: a long personal practice, a career change, or a studio asking them to deepen their skills. Whatever your background, you need a pathway that is structured, credible and grounded in real teaching experience.

Our Foundation Training programmes provide that structure. Delivered on site across Berkshire and Buckinghamshire, they combine anatomy, teaching methodology and supervised practicum so you graduate ready to lead classes with confidence.

Common starting points

Wherever you begin, we meet you there

We work with trainees at different stages of experience. Your path is shaped around your background, not a generic timetable.

Career changers

Moving into wellness teaching from another profession? We provide clear milestones, mentor support and practicum placements so you build competence alongside your existing commitments.

Experienced practitioners

Already teaching informally or assisting in classes? Foundation Training formalises your skills with Yoga Alliance Professionals certification and structured assessment.

Studio trainees

Studios partnering with TWA can offer trainees a credible certification route with on-site delivery, faculty oversight and pathways into teaching within their own timetable.

The pathway

What your training journey looks like

Foundation Training is not a single event. It is a progression from foundational knowledge through supervised teaching to professional qualification.

1. Enquire and assess fit

We discuss your experience, goals and availability. You receive honest guidance on the right entry level and cohort timing.

2. Foundation modules

Teacher-led sessions in anatomy, sequencing, ethics and class planning. Small cohorts with direct faculty contact throughout.

3. Supervised practicum

Practice teaching in supported environments with feedback from senior educators. This is where theory becomes confident delivery.

4. Certification and next steps

Graduate with Yoga Alliance Professionals recognition and a clear route into continued development or teaching opportunities.

FAQs

Your path questions

Practical answers for people considering teacher training.

Start your enquiry
Do I need years of practice before applying?

We recommend a consistent personal practice, but requirements vary by programme level. We will discuss your experience honestly during enquiry and advise on readiness.

Can I train while working full time?

Many trainees balance training with other commitments. Cohort schedules and modular formats are designed with working professionals in mind. Dates are confirmed per intake.

Is certification internationally recognised?

Foundation Training leads to Yoga Alliance Professionals certification from 200-hour through to 500-hour Advanced level.

What happens after I qualify?

Graduates can pursue CPD, specialist workshops and teaching opportunities through our studio and corporate network.
