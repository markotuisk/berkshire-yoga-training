Deepening expertise

Your Growth as a practitioner

Qualification is a milestone, not an endpoint. We help qualified teachers and wellbeing professionals stay current, deepen specialist skills and apply evidence-informed practice with confidence.

Overview

Professional development that stays practical

The wellness field evolves quickly. Anatomy research, client needs and professional standards all shift over time. Without structured development, even experienced teachers can feel uncertain about adapting classes or working with specialist populations.

Our CPD programmes and specialist workshops address that gap. Delivered on site by senior educators and clinical specialists, they focus on skills you can apply in your very next class or client session.

Development areas

Skills that strengthen your teaching

CPD and workshops cover the topics practitioners ask about most, grounded in movement science and holistic wellbeing.

Anatomy

Applied anatomy and biomechanics

Functional movement patterns, joint mechanics and sequencing decisions informed by how bodies actually move.

Adaptation

Pain-aware and inclusive teaching

Safe language, pacing and modifications for clients managing pain, fatigue or chronic conditions.

Breath work

Breath techniques in practice

Integrating diaphragmatic breath work safely into group classes and one-to-one sessions.

Specialist

Menopause and life-stage support

Evidence-informed approaches for teaching through hormonal transitions and changing client needs.

Trauma-informed

Trauma-aware class environments

Creating spaces where clients feel safe, with pacing and language that respects individual experience.

Wellbeing

Professional resilience

Tools for managing your own stress and boundaries while sustaining a long teaching career.

Your growth questions

Enquire about CPD
What is the difference between CPD and workshops?

CPD courses tend to be modular and cover core professional skills such as anatomy and pain-aware teaching. Workshops go deeper into specialist topics over shorter, focused sessions. Both are teacher-led and on site.

Can my studio book group CPD?

Yes. We deliver CPD for studio teams and corporate wellbeing leads. Contact us to discuss topics, group size and scheduling.

Does CPD count towards Yoga Alliance Professionals requirements?

CPD aligns with Yoga Alliance Professionals continuing education standards where applicable. Certification details are provided per course.

How do I stay informed about new courses?

Register your interest through Join or Contact and we will notify you when relevant courses are scheduled.
