How it works

Getting started is straightforward

From your first enquiry to certified graduation: three clear steps to begin your wellness education journey with us.

1. Enquire & apply

Tell us about your goals and experience. We will help you choose the right programme: Foundation Training, CPD or specialist workshops.

2. Train on site

Attend teacher-led sessions in Berkshire with supervised practicum, specialist modules and hands-on workshops in your chosen setting.

3. Graduate certified

Complete your certification with Yoga Alliance Professionals recognition and the confidence to teach safely across diverse populations.
