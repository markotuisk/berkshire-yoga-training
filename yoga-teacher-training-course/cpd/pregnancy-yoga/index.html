About this course

The Academy lists Pregnancy Yoga Training as a 50-hour CPD pathway. The March 2027 cohort is delivered across six modules on seven session days: six studio days and one self-study block. Studio teaching takes place in Berkshire and Reading; the exact venue is confirmed on enrolment.

Who it is for

Qualified yoga and wellness teachers who want to teach pregnancy-safe classes with clear professional boundaries.
