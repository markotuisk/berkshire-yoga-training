About this course

The Academy lists Functional Anatomy Training as a 30-hour CPD pathway. The June–July 2027 cohort currently listed on this page shows studio days and self-study blocks totalling 25 contact hours. Enquire if you need the full pathway hours confirmed for your intake. This page will not list Pregnancy, Nidra or Sound courses.

Who it is for

Qualified yoga and wellness teachers who want applied anatomy for safer class delivery.
