About this course

This CPD trains teachers to lead safe, simple Yoga Nidra practices with clear structure, invitational language and care for students in rest. The April 2027 cohort is a 30-hour pathway: Module 1 opens online, then studio days and guided self-study continue in Berkshire and Reading. The exact venue is confirmed on enrolment.

Who it is for

Qualified yoga and wellness teachers who want to facilitate Yoga Nidra and restorative rest with a professional container, not only as personal practice.

What you will cover

Six modules move from foundations to full teaching practice. Studio sessions include theory, skills and paired or group practice. Guided reading supports each stage.

  1. Foundations of rest and Yoga Nidra. Definitions of rest, sleep, meditation and Yoga Nidra; a brief structure of the practice; and an introduction to the neurology of rest and preparing a space for rest.
  2. History and styles. How sleep patterns and Yoga Nidra have been understood over time; prescriptive and invitational approaches; and skills in invitational language.
  3. Structure and safe holding. Settling, rotation, opposites, visualisation and return in more depth, plus how to hold a safe container as a facilitator.
  4. Opposites, visualisation and difficulty. Accessible imagery, managing trance-like states, and caring for students who dissociate or struggle to return.
  5. Creative applications and first full practice. Therapeutic and bespoke uses of Yoga Nidra, then recorded teaching of a full practice.
  6. Consolidation and review. Review of the pathway, further teaching practice, and reflection on your recorded session.

Reading

Course reading centres on Leading Safe and Simple Yoga Nidras, with chapter work between modules. Essential wider reading includes open research on Yoga Nidra history and the science of hypnagogic sleep. Further academic and optional titles are shared with enrolled trainees.

  • Birch and Hargreaves, Yoganidra: history and context
  • Eck, Behind the Veil of Hypnagogic Sleep (Harvard Medicine)
