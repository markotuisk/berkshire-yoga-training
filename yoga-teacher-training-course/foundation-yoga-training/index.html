200 hours

Foundation Teacher Training

Who it is for: people preparing to teach, or dedicated practitioners who accept assessment and practicum.

Outcome: plan and teach a mixed-level class with supervised practice, toward a Yoga Professionals Organisation Limited foundation pathway.

Teachers: Katia Major and Raili Maripuu (co-founders).

Early Bird £2,925 · Standard £3,250. Deposit 30% (Early Bird £877.50 · Standard £975). Nine weekends, 12 Feb to 14 Nov 2027.

300 hours

Advanced Yoga Teacher Training

Who it is for: students who want a broader teaching toolkit beyond the 200-hour Foundation.

Outcome: 200-hour Foundation plus 100-hour Advanced Extension (Modules 13 to 17): styles, advanced adjustments, applied anatomy, sequencing and practicum.

Teachers: Katia Major and Raili Maripuu (co-founders). Guest osteopath Yulia Wind.

Full pathway £3,850 (inclusive of TWA 200-hour Foundation TT). Advanced module dates TBD. Join at enrolment or upgrade during the Foundation, subject to space.

What you will learn

The 200-hour course covers techniques, teaching methodology, anatomy, philosophy and practicum. Exact hour splits are confirmed at enrolment. The dedicated 200-hour page has the full checklist.

  • Asana, sequencing and class craft for mixed-level students
  • Anatomy, physiology and biomechanics for safe teaching
  • Breath work, mindfulness and holistic wellbeing principles
  • Supervised teaching practice with faculty feedback
  • Professional standards aligned to Yoga Professionals Organisation Limited

Who it is for

Aspiring teachers beginning a professional pathway, and practitioners who want a structured, on-site certification route rather than fully automated learning.

Delivery

Sessions are teacher-led and delivered on site across Berkshire. Cohort sizes stay intentional so feedback remains personal.

Fees

200-hour Foundation Teacher Training: Early Bird £2,925 · Standard £3,250. Deposit to secure your place: 30% of the fee (Early Bird £877.50 · Standard £975). Balance due before the course starts; instalment details confirmed in your offer letter. Cohort: 12 February to 14 November 2027.
