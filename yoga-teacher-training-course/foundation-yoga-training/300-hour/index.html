Course structure

Students on the 300-hour pathway complete the same 200-hour Foundation, then take an additional 100-hour Advanced Extension. The Advanced Extension does not replace or repeat the Foundation. It builds on it and takes key areas further.

  • 300-hour pathway: 200-hour Foundation + 80 additional contact hours + 20 additional non-contact hours = 300 hours
  • Join from the start when you enrol, or upgrade partway through the 200-hour Foundation, subject to space on the Advanced modules
  • 500-hour pathways beyond this award: TBD · Ask the Academy

Build a bigger teaching toolkit

The pathway includes the complete 200-hour Foundation plus an additional 100 hours of advanced training for teachers who want a broader, more adaptable classroom craft.

  • Takes hands-on adjustments beyond the Foundation, with more extensive supervised practice and greater attention to individual bodies and presentations
  • Explores a wider range of yoga styles and methodologies, and when different approaches may be useful
  • Develops a broader toolkit for sequencing, adapting classes and teaching different populations and needs
  • Deepens applied anatomy, movement science, pain science and safe modification
  • Builds confidence in developing your own teaching voice, style and areas of special interest

300-hour Advanced Extension

The Advanced Extension assumes the knowledge and skills of the 200-hour Foundation. It focuses on application, comparison and deeper practice rather than repeating Foundation material. Exact calendar dates, session times and venues for Modules 13 to 17 are TBD. Ask the Academy when you enquire.

Module 13 · The many paths of yoga (16 contact hours)

Styles, approaches and methodologies. Expands understanding beyond the Foundation and develops the ability to draw intelligently from different approaches.

  • Comparing Ashtanga, Vinyasa, Hatha, Yin, Restorative and other traditional and contemporary approaches
  • Different approaches to sequencing, alignment, breath, pace and use of props
  • Understanding the intention behind different styles rather than treating them as competing systems
  • How different styles may suit different students, venues, settings and goals
  • Building a personal teaching toolkit without losing coherence or authenticity
  • Teaching the same theme or movement through different stylistic lenses

Module 14 · Advanced adjustments and asana lab (16 contact hours)

Extends the Foundation adjustment clinic into more confident, individualised hands-on work.

  • Advanced adjustment principles across standing and seated poses, twists, backbends and inversions
  • Reading individual anatomy, resistance, compensation and range of motion
  • Using touch to support rather than force movement
  • Psychological awareness and trauma-informed teaching
  • Adjustments for different bodies, ages, abilities and teaching environments
  • Extended supervised practice with peer and guest-student feedback
  • Consent, communication, boundaries and when not to adjust

Module 15 · Applied anatomy, modification and real students (16 contact hours)

Takes the Foundation's anatomy and pain science into more complex teaching situations.

  • Deeper exploration of individual variation and movement options
  • Common injuries and pre-existing conditions: presentation, adaptation and appropriate referral
  • Principles of rehabilitation and safe loading through asana
  • Building a practical modification toolkit rather than relying on fixed contraindication lists
  • Working with chronic pain, older students, pregnancy and postnatal students
  • Integrating nervous-system-aware language into teaching

Module 16 · Advanced sequencing and the teaching toolkit (16 contact hours)

Develops the ability to design purposeful classes for different students, styles and settings.

  • Sequencing around a theme, intention, movement pattern or student need
  • Designing mixed-ability and specialist classes
  • Using different teaching methodologies: demonstration, verbal cueing, observation and hands-on support
  • Developing class arcs, transitions and intelligent progression
  • Finding and refining your own teaching voice and style
  • Peer teaching, observation and structured feedback

Module 17 · Specialisms, practicum and integration (16 contact hours)

Brings the additional learning together and gives students the opportunity to apply their expanded toolkit.

  • Student selection of one area of interest, for example Yin Yoga, Yoga Nidra, pranayama or movement-based practice, and run an example class
  • Adapting a class for a specific population, intention or setting
  • Creating a class that draws appropriately from more than one yoga methodology
  • Teaching a full class for assessment and receiving structured feedback
  • Reflecting on strengths, development areas and future CPD priorities
  • Creating a personal teaching development plan for continued learning

Additional non-contact hours · 20 hours

  • Advanced reading and reflective journalling
  • Written assignments comparing styles, methodologies and teaching approaches
  • Observation of teachers working in different yoga styles
  • Advanced teaching or practicum log and reflective review
  • Preparation and reflection for the final teaching assessment

Assessment and certification

Assessment continues through the Advanced Extension, with emphasis on application: observation, supervised and in-depth adjustment practice, comparative work across yoga styles, sequencing, adaptation and teaching. The final assessment brings together the expanded teaching toolkit in a full class and reflective portfolio.

On successful completion of the full pathway, graduates receive the Thames Wellness Academy 300-Hour Certificate. Pass criteria detail and any external accreditation wording: TBD · Ask the Academy.

FAQ

Does the 300-hour include the 200-hour Foundation?

Yes. The published £3,850 full pathway fee is inclusive of the TWA 200-hour Foundation Teacher Training. The Advanced Extension then adds 100 hours.

Can I join only the Advanced Extension?

You can join the Advanced pathway at enrolment, or upgrade during the 200-hour Foundation subject to space on the Advanced modules. Pricing if you already hold a 200-hour certificate from elsewhere, or upgrade mid-cohort only: TBD · Ask the Academy.

When do the Advanced modules run?

Exact dates, times and venues for Modules 13 to 17 are TBD. Ask the Academy when you enquire. The Foundation weekends for the 2027 cohort are published on the 200-hour page.
