FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Common questions about our teacher-led programmes, certification and how to get started with Thames Wellness Academy.

What programmes does Thames Wellness Academy offer?

We offer Yoga Alliance Professionals certified Foundation Training, continuing professional development (CPD), specialist workshops, retreats and research collaborations. All programmes are teacher-led and delivered on site.

Are your courses certified?

Yes. Thames Wellness Academy is certified by Yoga Alliance Professionals. Our Foundation Training pathways meet recognised standards for professional yoga and wellness teaching.

Where are programmes delivered?

Our programmes are delivered on site across Berkshire, including dedicated training venues and partner studio locations. Specific venue details are shared when you enquire or apply.

How do I apply for Foundation Training?

Visit our Join page to submit an application or enquiry. Tell us about your experience and goals, and our team will guide you through the next steps, including course dates and entry requirements.

Do you offer CPD for qualified teachers?

Yes. Our CPD programmes support qualified teachers and wellness professionals who wish to deepen their practice in anatomy, breath work, therapeutic teaching and evidence-informed approaches.

Can organisations book workshops or training?

We work with studios, corporate wellbeing teams and healthcare settings to deliver bespoke workshops and training. Contact us with your requirements and we will discuss tailored options.
