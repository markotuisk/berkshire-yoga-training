## Site overview

### Purpose

Thames Wellness Academy delivers professional wellness education in Berkshire and Buckinghamshire. The website promotes Yoga Alliance Professionals-certified teacher training, continuing professional development (CPD), specialist workshops, retreats and research collaboration.

All programmes are teacher-led and delivered on site across studio, corporate and therapeutic settings. The site is designed to attract aspiring teachers, qualified practitioners, corporate wellbeing teams and research partners.

### Target audience

- Aspiring yoga and wellness teachers seeking Foundation Training certification
- Qualified teachers and therapists pursuing CPD
- Wellness professionals interested in specialist workshops
- Corporate wellbeing leads and healthcare settings booking training
- Academic and clinical collaborators for research programmes
- Prospective students in Berkshire, Buckinghamshire and the wider Thames Valley

### Geographic focus

Programmes are delivered on site across **Berkshire** and **Buckinghamshire**, including training venues in Reading, Windsor and Newbury. Content references the Thames Valley region and on-site delivery in studio, corporate and therapeutic environments.

### Brand

- **Primary name:** Thames Wellness Academy (TWA)
- **Project / repository name:** Berkshire Yoga Training
- **Certification body:** Yoga Alliance Professionals

**Headline:** Foundation Training

**Lead:** Internationally recognised teacher training from 200-hour Foundation to 500-hour Advanced level. Delivered on site across Berkshire and Buckinghamshire.

- Overview of Yoga Alliance Professionals certification pathways

- Curriculum covers anatomy, physiology, biomechanics, mindfulness, breath work and supervised practicum

- Suitable for aspiring and experienced teachers across corporate, studio and therapeutic settings
